## 1. Objective

Register a new top-level "Setup" tab (order 60) with PillBar sub-navigation containing three pills:
1. **Apps** — Strava OAuth configuration (client_id, client_secret, authorize button)
2. **Level Mapping** — Initial calibration wizard (refactored from `calibration-wizard.js`)
3. **Power Tuning** — Recalibration workflow (refactored from `recalibration.js`)

The queue status display currently in `upload_settings.js` will be split out — Strava OAuth config stays as the "Apps" pill; the upload queue status moves to the `/ride` page (WO-15.3's responsibility, not this WO's).
