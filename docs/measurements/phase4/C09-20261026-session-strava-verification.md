### Memory Budget Notes (`SPEC.md` §3.4, §9.3, §10.2, §10.6)
- **Active Ride Ring Buffer (`RideStore::allocateBuffer()`):** Allocates `14,400 * sizeof(RideRecord) = 115,200 B` (`112.5 KB`, 4 hours at 1 Hz) in PSRAM via `heap_caps_malloc(..., MALLOC_CAP_SPIRAM | MALLOC_CAP_8BIT)` on ESP32-S3, falling back to `3,600 * 8 = 28,800 B` (`28.1 KB`, 1 hour) in internal DRAM only if PSRAM is unavailable.
- **Streaming TCX & Gzip Scratch (`TCXGenerator`):** Strictly bounded to a single `4,096 B` (`4 KB`) formatting buffer (`kScratchBufferSize = 4096`) plus streaming `miniz` (`tdefl_compressor`) deflation directly to LittleFS or HTTP response stream — zero full-ride XML strings in RAM.
- **TLS + BLE Coexistence (`UploadQueue`):** Each outbound HTTPS call (`StravaUploader::refreshTokenIfNeeded`, `postUpload`, `pollUploadStatus`) is executed inside a single split stage on the background `DataCollector` FreeRTOS task (`Core 0`) while holding a stack-allocated `BLEManager::ScanInhibit` RAII guard (`#ifdef WITH_BLE`) so internal SRAM retains $> 40\text{ KB}$ headroom during `mbedTLS` handshakes.

## 3. On-Bench Interactive Verification Checklist (`SPEC.md` §13.7)

Execute the following procedure when the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Nano is connected via USB-C and reachable at `http://192.168.1.100` (or `http://echenon.local`):

### Step 1 — Flash & Boot Verification
1. Flash production or diagnostic firmware via PlatformIO MCP (`upload` for environment `ex3-waveshare-nano` or `ex3-waveshare-nano-diag`).
2. Confirm boot log reports PSRAM ride buffer allocation (`14,400` records = `115,200 B`), LittleFS mount (`/rides` directory ready), and registration of `Session` (order `80`) and `Upload Services` (order `90`) portal tabs.

### Step 2 — Session Auto-Start, Manual Controls, Coasting & Auto-End (`Tests 4.1–4.4, 4.9`)
1. **Auto-Start (`4.1`):** With `auto_start_enabled: true` (`GET /api/session/status`), pedal continuously for `> 5 s`. Verify `/ws/echenon/live` transitions `session_state: "IDLE" -> "ACTIVE"` and `session_elapsed_s` increments at 1 Hz.
2. **Coasting vs. Manual Pause (`4.2, 4.9`):**
   - Stop pedalling for `15 s` while `ACTIVE` (coasting): verify `session_state` remains `"ACTIVE"` and `0 W / 0 RPM` records are logged at 1 Hz.
   - Click **Pause** in the Session tab (`POST /api/session/pause`): verify `session_state` becomes `"PAUSED"`. Wait `15 s`, then click **Resume** (`POST /api/session/resume`) and pedal for another `30 s`.
3. **Inactivity Auto-End (`4.4`) or Manual End (`4.2`):** Stop pedalling for `180 s` (or click **End Session**). Verify trailing zero-cadence records from the final stop are stripped and the completed ride appears in `GET /api/session/history`.
4. **Subsequent Ride After `ENDED` (`4.1`):** Resume pedalling for `> 5 s` without rebooting; verify `SessionManager` transitions cleanly from `ENDED -> ACTIVE` with a fresh session ID.
5. **Manual Discard (`4.2`):** Start a short session, click **Discard**, and verify `session_state` returns to `"IDLE"` and `/ride_active.bin` is removed without adding an entry to `GET /api/session/history`.

### Step 3 — Crash Recovery & 5-Ride FIFO Eviction (`Test 4.6`)
1. Pedal for `> 65 s` (`>= 60` 1 Hz records, crossing at least two `30 s` LittleFS checkpoints to `/ride_active.bin`), then press hardware reset (or power-cycle USB-C) mid-ride.
2. On reboot, query `GET /api/session/history` and confirm `RideStore::recoverInterruptedRide()` finalized the interrupted ride into `/rides/ride_<id>.bin`.
3. Complete a 6th stored ride and verify `RideStore` evicts the oldest `.bin` (and any `.tcx.gz`), maintaining a maximum of `5` stored rides.

### Step 4 — Authenticated TCX Download & Xert / Garmin Schema Validation (`Tests 4.7–4.9`)
1. Verify unauthenticated `curl -i "http://192.168.1.100/api/session/download?id=<id>&format=tcx"` returns `HTTP/1.1 401 Unauthorized`.
2. Authenticate via the portal and download `/api/session/download?id=<id>&format=tcx` and `format=tcx.gz`.
3. Inspect the downloaded `.tcx` file:
   - Confirm `<TrainingCenterDatabase>` root schema and `<Creator><Name>Echenon EX-3</Name></Creator>`.
   - Confirm 1 Hz `<Trackpoint>` elements contain `<Time>`, `<DistanceMeters>`, `<Cadence>`, `<HeartRateBpm>` (when BLE HR connected), and `<Extensions><ns3:TPX><ns3:Speed>...</ns3:Speed><ns3:Watts>...</ns3:Watts></ns3:TPX></Extensions>`.
   - Confirm the `15 s` coasting window contains continuous 1 Hz `<Trackpoint>` entries with `<ns3:Watts>0</ns3:Watts>` and `<ns3:Speed>0.00</ns3:Speed>`, whereas the `15 s` manual `PAUSED` window has no `<Trackpoint>` timestamps during the pause gap.

### Step 5 — Live Strava BYOA OAuth 2.0 & Background Upload (`Tests 4.10–4.14`)
1. Open the **Upload Services** tab (`#upload_settings`), enter Strava API `Client ID` and `Client Secret`, and click **Save Credentials** (`POST /api/strava/credentials`).
2. Click **Connect with Strava** (`GET /api/strava/auth-url`), authorize the app on Strava, and confirm redirect back to `/api/strava/callback?code=...&state=...` succeeds and `GET /api/strava/status` reports `"token_valid": true`.
3. Replay the same `/api/strava/callback` URL in the browser and confirm it returns `HTTP 403 Forbidden` (single-use `state` consumed).
